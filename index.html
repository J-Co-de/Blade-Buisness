HOW IT WORKS

Our Process

  • 01

    Drop Off or Ship

    Bring blades to our workshop or ship them in a hard case. We inspect every edge on arrival.

  • 02

    Diagnosis & Quote

    We photograph the blade, assess damage, and send you a written quote before touching a stone.

  • 03

    Restoration Work

    Hand-ground on diamond plates and finished on water stones to 8000 grit. No shortcuts.

  • 04

    Quality Check

    Every edge is tested on newsprint and leather before it leaves the bench. We sign off on each one.

  • 05

    Return

    Pick up or receive back via insured mail, wrapped in oil cloth. Turnaround 3–7 business days.
